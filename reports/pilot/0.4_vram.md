Reading the numbers
- Steady residency of the whole stack is small (7.4 GiB); the budget is set by LatentSync's inference transient
  (~17.3 GiB on top of the resident set, 20.5 GiB when alone). After one LatentSync run the torch caching allocator keeps
  ~21.9 GiB reserved inside the process; the resident process therefore settles at ~24.7 GiB, still 7.7 GiB under the card.
- Loading LatentSync after the audio models costs only 1.3 GiB incremental (shared CUDA context / cuDNN / cuBLAS handles)
  versus 2.9 GiB when loaded first - simultaneous residency is cheaper than the isolated numbers suggest.
- Audio-side inference while everything is resident adds < 0.4 GiB (whisper 7776 vs 7446 steady).
- What is NOT covered here: two LatentSync jobs at once, or LatentSync on longer/higher-resolution inputs than the demo
  (transient scales with num_frames x resolution). Both would eat into the 7.7 GiB headroom and should be re-measured
  in 0.5 with the real pilot videos; with one job at a time the resident layout holds.
