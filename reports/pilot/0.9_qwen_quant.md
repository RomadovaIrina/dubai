Observations for the manual reviewer (not a verdict):
- en-ru-09 (the long sentence): **both** quants switch to Chinese halfway through — a Qwen behaviour, not a quantization
  artefact; the production prompt/decoding must guard against it (e.g. stop on CJK, retry, or split long sentences).
- Q5-only glitches: en-de-01 contains a CJK glyph inside a German word; en-de-10 "niederlasse" (ungrammatical) vs Q8
  "niederbeugte"; en-ru-03 keeps "paginated" in English where Q8 writes "пагинированый" (itself misspelled).
- Both quants: en-de-06 leaves "due" untranslated; ambiguous sentences ("I saw her duck", "Косил косой косой косой")
  are rendered literally by both.
- Numbers/dates/currency survive in all 6 number sentences for both quants.
- Within-quant repeats at temperature 0 occasionally differ in wording (llama.cpp multi-threaded reduction order);
  the CSV keeps the first repeat.

## Verdict

```text
speed winner:     Q5_K_M   (median 7.99 vs 5.40 tok/s, 1.48x; load 2.6 s vs 12.4 s; 2.5 GiB less RAM)
quality decision: Q8_0 selected after manual review of reports/pilot/0.9_qwen_translations.csv (2026-09-18)
selected quant:   Q8_0
0.9 status:       CLOSED
```

Manual-review outcome (project decision, raw benchmark numbers above are unchanged):
- Q5_K_M is faster (~1.48x median generation speed), but Q8_0 was chosen as the production quant because its
  translations are slightly, yet more consistently, better: Q5_K_M showed more explicit quality/format defects
  (mixed-script corruption / stray CJK glyphs inside words, a few weaker formulations), most visibly on EN->DE.
- 21/36 outputs were identical between the two quants.
- Errors present in BOTH quants (e.g. the long en-ru sentence switching to Chinese, "due" left untranslated) are
  model/prompt issues, not quantization-specific, and are out of scope for this decision.
- For the dubbing pipeline translation quality is preferred over CPU speed: Qwen2.5-7B-Instruct Q8_0, CPU
  (n_gpu_layers=0). The clean E2E runner (scripts/pilot/run_clean_pipeline_05.py) and scripts/download_models.sh use
  Q8_0 since this decision; q4_k_m is no longer part of the pipeline.

Not changed: torch / CUDA / environment. Q4_K_M (the smoke quant) was used only for a 4-sentence dry run of the harness
and is not part of the result.
