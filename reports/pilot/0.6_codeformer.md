Notes
- Timing is GPU-exclusive (nothing else on the card; 2 MiB after every process) but the CPU quota is 13.6 vCPU:
  LatentSync's affine transform / face restore stages and CodeFormer's per-frame detection + PNG writing are CPU-bound,
  so a wider CPU quota would move both numbers; the GPU-side UNet loop is the dominant LatentSync cost.
- CodeFormer's 0.148 s/frame includes writing cropped/restored/final PNGs to disk and re-encoding, as in the
  production script; the pure network forward is a small part of it (0.4 GiB, 8 faces in 0.23 s in the 0.4 probe).
- The 300 s/min question is answered by measurement: with the current production config the video contour needs
  ~625 s per source minute on the 5090. Levers (not evaluated here, out of 0.6 scope): fewer steps / DeepCache
  interval, batching CodeFormer faces, skipping PNG dumps, running CodeFormer only on changed regions.
