## Verdict

```text

reason: 3 of 5 real-content videos (01, 02, 05) cannot be processed by upstream LatentSync as delivered —
        "Face not detected" (no face on screen in 01/02 for most or all frames; face below LatentSync's 80 px
        minimum in 9 % of 05's frames). SyncNet also finds no face in original 02.
measured: 2/5 LatentSync outputs, 4/5 originals
```

What this means for the pilot (decision, not measurement): the current pipeline assumes a face in every frame. Real
customer content mixes talking-head with B-roll and picture-in-picture. Unblocking needs a product/pipeline choice,
e.g. pass-through of face-less segments (lipsync only the tracked face segments, copy the rest) and/or a lower face
size threshold or cropping to the PiP window for 05. Such a change is outside 0.8's scope and was not made here.
