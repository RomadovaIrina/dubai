## Notes

- **Duration validation:** every assembled and final output matches the source duration to 0.000 s (tolerance max(0.10 s, 2 frames)):
  chunks are cut with pcm audio into .mov, so boundaries are sample-accurate and the concat demuxer reproduces the exact sum; the aac
  encode at burn time adds no drift. Video/audio streams present, resolution preserved, chunk union covers [0, duration] with no gaps.
- **VFR sources (01 avg 25.0, 02 29.98, 05 29.92 fps):** the frame-accurate re-encode writes CFR 30 fps chunks (ffmpeg default), so the
  finals have more frames than the sources (01: 2542 -> 3050, 02: 3324 -> 3326, 05: 1567 -> 1571) by duplication, never by dropping;
  timeline and duration are exact. Recorded, not hidden; no fps change was requested.
- **Subtitles:** with --asr the cues are faster-whisper segment timestamps on the original timeline (first/last cue in each
  0.11_stages_XX.json); without --asr they would be the VAD speech intervals. ASR time is excluded from S.
- **Informational copy mode (04.mp4, 2 runs):** S = 3.6274 s (4.038 s/min) but the assembled clip is 72.1737 s for a 53.90 s
  source (Δ +18.27 s): keyframe-snapped cuts extend each of the 31 chunks backwards to a keyframe, so chunks overlap and validation FAILS.
  Not a contractual result — `reencode` is the official mode. (`reports/pilot/0.11_stages_04_copy.json`)
- **Where the time goes:** T_vad_split is 55–65 % of S and is dominated by the ~30–60 per-chunk libx264 re-encodes, not by Silero
  (VAD itself is ~0.1 s); T_subtitle_burn is a full-length libx264 pass; T_concat is a stream copy.
