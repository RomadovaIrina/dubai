## 1. Bottom line

The generated lower face is mis-registered by three generator-input effects. All three are on my side and all three are fixable:

| # | cause | evidence | fix | cost |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| A | **LatentSync's `AlignRestore` runs in fp16** (crop warp, paste-back warp and the inverse matrix; translations of ~300-500 px quantised to 0.25-0.5 px) — a fresh registration error of the whole pasted face on EVERY frame, camera shake or not | with NO generation (crop -> paste only) the nose base already slides 0.12-0.27 px/frame in fp16 vs **0.013-0.044 px in fp32** (01 / 03 / 04 / 05) | `AlignRestore` in float32 (`--affine-precision fp32`) | none measurable (LS s equal within noise) |
| B | **crop anchors lag the head** (clean0 ±2 box filter follows only 12-30 % of the high-frequency head motion; on 04's shaky seconds the crop misses the head by 4.0 px mean / 10 px p95) | LatentSync follows the crop CONTENT well: the generated face's offset follows only ~2 % of the head shake (gain −0.022), but on 04 that is still visible in the numbers: fp32 alone 0.157 px -> flow anchors + fp32 **0.114 px** (cheeks 0.256 -> **0.140**) | **flow-driven anchors**: rigid upper-face motion from DIS optical flow, detector noise smoothed only around that trajectory (`--anchor-filter flow:4`) | rigid-motion pass ~32 s per video-minute CPU (4 threads, measured under load), depends only on the master -> runs in parallel with the audio stage like the MediaPipe pass |
| C | **the composite mask ellipse lags the head** (clean0 mask box = ±3 frame average) -> the soft boundary between generated and source skin slides over the cheeks / nose base | ellipse centre vs head motion on 04 w1: clean0 **4.5 px/frame mean, 11.6 p95**; mask from the flow trajectory **1.5 / 3.3** (03: 0.60 -> 0.41-0.52) | mask boxes from the SAME flow-corrected trajectory, no extra smoothing (automatic with `--anchor-filter`; refinement `--mask-track head` = head-locked box, running) | none |

## 2. How it was measured

* **Shaky windows** (`../shaky_windows.json`, `scripts/quality/anchor_study.py`): per second, high-pass energy of the rigid upper-face
  motion (DIS flow + RANSAC similarity on brows / eyes / glasses / nose bridge; no detector in the motion), top seconds inside the
  baseline LatentSync segments. 04 is by far the shakiest (head high-pass 4-5 px/frame rms vs 0.7 on 03, 1.3 on 05).
* **Slide** (`scripts/quality/slide_metric.py`): the output frame and the master frame show the same head at the same place, so DIS flow
  master(t) -> output(t), median over the nose base (alae / nostrils / subnasale / philtrum top) and nasolabial cheek discs inside the
  composite mask = the registration error of the generated lower face. Slide = its change between consecutive frames (what the eye sees
  as sliding). Noise floor on the untouched upper face: 0.011-0.017 px.
* **No-generation arms** (`--mode wrapper`: crop -> paste with the same geometry, no UNet) isolate the geometry chain from the generator.
* **Validation of the rigid motion** (it drives the flow anchors and the mask metric): on 04 it explains the high-pass eye motion from
  3.7 px to 0.6 px rms and the remainder is uncorrelated with the shake (−0.08).
* Common metric v4 (`mouth_jitter_hr.py`, copied from experiment/restoration-temporal) on every arm vs the master window: lower-face
  shear 0.94-1.01 and mouth resid 0.92-1.02 in all lip-synced arms, no arm adds a seam or texture flicker. SyncNet: dub-only + final mix.

## 4. Speed

fp32 AlignRestore: no measurable LatentSync cost. Flow anchors: rigid-motion pass 28.6 s for 04 (53.9 s) with 4 threads = ~32 s per
video-minute on a loaded box (upper bound), filter 0.4 s; CPU only, off the critical path (parallel to the audio stage, like MediaPipe).
MediaPipe every 3rd frame (equal slide) brings the face-stack pass from 44 to ~15 s/video-min. LatentSync s/video-min of this round's
window runs are NOT valid (container CPU quota 17.5 cores shared with the v4 / SyncNet evals, LS time up to 2x); stage 2 measures it clean.
