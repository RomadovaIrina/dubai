# m15 (clean) vs m14 (CodeFormer + InsightFace), direct comparison: 01, 03, 05

Both use audio-r8-v4 (audio of the right-hand video) and the same composite: fallback fb6 + inner-mouth deflicker dfk2. Only the face models
differ. Left = m15_clean, right = m14_codeformer.

Files (each <= 25 MB):
- <id>_m15_vs_m14_sbs.mp4: full frame.
- <id>_m15_vs_m14_zoom_sbs.mp4: face zoom (03 2x; 01 and 05 ~2.7-3x because their faces are ~90-100 px).

Columns:
- SyncNet = dub-speech confidence (gate metric, higher is better).
- Flashes = strong lip flashes (lower is better).
- Flicker = inner-mouth frame-to-frame HF luma / flicker events per s (lower is better).
- Pressed lips = s of closed mouth while the source mouth is open and the dub is silent.
- Teeth / cavity = share of bright (teeth) pixels inside the open mouth / median cavity luma / % of open frames with a near-black hole;
  source in brackets.

Reading:
- m14 is slightly closer to the source on teeth and black holes: 03 teeth 0.008 vs 0.003 (source 0.007); fewer black-hole frames on 01, 03
  and 05.
- m14 is better on 05 (SyncNet +0.37, fewer flashes, less flicker).
- m14 is worse on 01 (SyncNet -0.8) and on 03 pressed lips (4.2 vs 2.0 s); 03 SyncNet is equal.
- m14 mouth / dub mismatch is higher on 03 / 05 (2.63 / 2.50 vs 1.70 / 0.97 s).
- No clear overall winner by the numbers; the user's eye decides the look.
