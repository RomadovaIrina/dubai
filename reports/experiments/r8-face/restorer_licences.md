# CodeFormer replacement: licence check (lip/mouth region, small faces)

Checked 2026-10-29 against primary sources (raw LICENSE files, READMEs, GitHub release API, HF/ModelScope cards). This is not legal advice.

**FFHQ:** NVIDIA releases the dataset (metadata, scripts, docs) under "Creative Commons BY-NC-SA 4.0". The individual images carry their own Flickr licences: CC BY 2.0, CC BY-NC 2.0, Public Domain Mark 1.0, CC0 1.0 or U.S. Government Works (https://github.com/NVlabs/ffhq-dataset). Nobody has settled in law whether weights trained on NC data inherit the NC term. Below, "grey" means a permissive code/weight licence with FFHQ training data. Risk is low to moderate: it is widely used in commercial tools, but no court has tested it.

**Shared helpers (facexlib, MIT code)** (https://raw.githubusercontent.com/xinntao/facexlib/master/LICENSE): the code licences of its components are permissive, but the weights are not all clean.
- Detection comes from Pytorch_Retinaface (MIT; weights trained on WIDER FACE, whose terms are UNVERIFIED because the site failed TLS).
- Parsing comes from face-parsing.PyTorch (MIT). That BiSeNet, and probably ParseNet too, is trained on **CelebAMask-HQ: "available for non-commercial research purposes only"**.
- Recognition comes from InsightFace_Pytorch (MIT), matting from MODNet ("CC 4.0", variant unstated), tracking from SORT (GPL 3.0).

GFPGAN's `FaceRestoreHelper` uses the RetinaFace + parsing weights by default.

## Recommendation
1. No face-specific restorer with a fully clean data chain was found. Every face prior here (GFPGAN, RestoreFormer(++), GPEN, VQFR) is FFHQ-trained, so all of them are **grey**. The current Real-ESRGAN is grey too (DIV2K is research-only).
2. Best candidate for the lip crop: **GFPGAN v1.4 (clean arch)**. It has Apache-2.0 code, a single 349 MB GitHub-release weight, no NVIDIA CUDA ops, it is fast and deterministic per frame, and it is the most widely deployed (so its risk profile is best understood). Use it only through `GFPGANv1Clean`. Never use the v1 paper model.
3. Bypass facexlib's detection/parsing weights (CelebAMask-HQ NC). Align the face with the landmarks LatentSync already produces, and paste back with your own feathered mouth mask. Blend GFPGAN at a partial weight (e.g. 0.3–0.6) to limit identity drift and temporal flicker on the mouth.
4. If legal wants zero FFHQ/DIV2K exposure, use **RealBasicVSR or BasicVSR++ (REDS, CC BY 4.0, Apache-2.0)** on the mouth crop. They are generic and temporally consistent, but they do not hallucinate teeth or lip detail the way a face prior does.
5. Avoid DifFace, PGDiff, DMDNet (NC), the DiffBIR face pipeline (NC stage-1, plus a CodeFormer checkpoint in its bundle) and GPEN's GitHub repo (no licence).
