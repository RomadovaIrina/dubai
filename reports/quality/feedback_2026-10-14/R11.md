## 3. What happens, stage by stage

Audio (Qwen2.5-7B Q8_0 on the CPU, Chatterbox v3 fp16 on the GPU):
1. VAD, Whisper, Qwen punctuation -> sentence units (over-long sentences split at a clause boundary), diarization, Qwen translation (whole sentence).
2. Chatterbox: ONE generation of the whole English sentence, TTS QA (Whisper + CTC, `--tts-word-qa`: a lost content word or a swallowed first
   word -> new seed), CTC word alignment. `--fit-up off`: no Qwen "fuller" padding ("That's great.", "it is.").
3. Overflow (sentence longer than its window up to the next sentence): Qwen concise rewrite above 1.1x, else atempo <= 1.1.
4. **Fill** (`--thought-fill spoken`): Qwen marks the meaning chunks, the chunk plan predicts where the dub ends; if it ends >= 0.8 s before the
   speaker, (a) Qwen re-translates in his fuller spoken wording (generic few-shots: "опять же" -> "once again", discourse words kept, nothing added,
   Qwen faithfulness judge), (b) else Chatterbox re-speaks the same text at `cfg_weight` 0.3 (the model's own slower delivery; rejected on a drawn-out
   word or a > 0.7 s pause inside a phrase). Accepted only if the end moves >= 0.3 s closer and the TTS QA passes.
5. **Meaning chunks** (`--underfill semantic`, `thought_policy.chunk_marks` + `plan_chunks`): Qwen splits the English sentence into the largest
   self-contained meaning chunks (never after the / a / to / of / for / with / our / how / what / that ... unless punctuated); the DP puts each chunk
   on the start of one of the speaker's bursts so the dub covers as much of his speech as possible (a chunk may start on a later burst; dub over
   source silence costs little — the reviewer only minds the speaker talking while the dub is silent).
6. Cuts in the silent run between words, part ends where the energy really decays (no clipped final consonants); placement at natural speed,
   slowed to >= 0.9 only to end with the speaker (`--thought-slow-floor 0.9`); 0.2 s minimum pause between sentences.
7. Background: HDemucs bed at the original voice/background balance, **no ducking** under the dub (`--bg-duck-db 0`), the HDemucs hole under the
   source speech filled with the video's own room tone up to -30 dBFS.

Video (LatentSync 1.6 + CodeFormer):
8. Gate = source speech U dub speech (+0.24 s, gaps < 1.2 s merged); **`--silent-mouth self`**: LatentSync's reference is the frame itself
   everywhere, so where the dub is silent and the speaker talks the generated mouth follows his articulation (no closed / frozen mouth on a talking
   jaw — that is what produced every seam / ghost of R1 / R6-R10).
9. Mouth-only paste-back (feathered lower-face ellipse), CodeFormer on the LatentSync face before the composite (eyes / brows stay the source),
   landmark smoothing of LatentSync's affine crop.

## 5. Known limits / next

1. Where the speaker still talks after the English ended (04: 7.7 s, mostly sentence ends of a slow speaker), the mouth moves without sound
   (source articulation). The fill gave little on 04 (8.3 -> 7.7 s): the translations are already faithful, the speaker is simply slower.
2. Time: 562 s per video-minute on 04 (TTS stage 185 s: chunk marks + fill rewrites + judge + extra TTS takes; LatentSync 203 s on 999 frames
   because the gate covers all source speech again). Speed work after the quality is accepted (Qwen calls can be batched / cached, the
   pace take is often rejected and could be skipped when the rewrite was accepted).
3. Not measured for R11 yet: WER / CER, SyncNet (also `syncnet_speech_only.py`), 05 / 02.
4. Qwen chunk marks are sometimes marginal ("Sochi city |" without a comma); `chunk_marks` drops marks after function words only.
